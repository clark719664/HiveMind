Honest status. Quorum Quest is an educational toy built on the Swarm Lab engine: an analogy of effects measured in the real E13/E14 experiments, not a reproduction of them. Its numbers must never be quoted as results about real LLM training, and no peer review of record exists for the project. The score is a learning score, a teaching number: it is not PTS or $HIVE and earns nothing. No network, no accounts, no multiplayer.

Quorum Quest: you are the swarm coordinator

Four workers train on their own data and send you their deltas each round. You decide how many to wait for (K), what to do with late deltas, how long to wait after the K-th arrival (grace) and how to merge. Your learning score is 100 × retention of the synchronous improvement (capped at 1), minus a time penalty on the time-budget level. Best total so far: 0 (stored only in this browser).
