# 🤝 Project HiveMind — Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, gamers, ML researchers, and maintainers of **Project HiveMind** pledge to make participation in our open-source peer development community and the **`HiveMind: Neural Breach` (Free Steam Edition)** swarm a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
- Sharing reproducible **8 GB–12 GB GPU benchmarks**, honest VRAM telemetry, and constructive code reviews.
- Designing clean, modular Pull Requests that respect the **8 GB consumer GPU VRAM ceiling** and **deterministic CUDA execution**.
- Helping newcomers onboard onto **Phase 2 "Swarm Fabric" (M1–M4)** tasks and **Hugging Face Domain Expert** missions.
- Competing fairly on the **Steamworks Global Leaderboard** without Sybil spoofing or gradient poisoning (leave Byzantine attacks to the unit tests!).

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Submitting malicious weights, backdoored scripts, or intentionally poisoned checkpoints outside of designated Byzantine test harnesses.
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments in Issues, Pull Requests, or Community Channels.
- Publishing others' private information or API credentials (`HF_TOKEN`, Steam session tickets, etc.).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub or `callensxavier@gmail.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
